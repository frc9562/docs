## Requirements for the Robot
- Robot must not have a **Perimeter** greater than **110.0in (2.794m)** outer most parts (Excluding Bumpers)
- Robot must not be more then **30in (76.2cm) tall**
	- Robot can not extend 30in (76.2cm) in height no matter what.
	- The **Trench** is 22.25in (56.52cm) Above the Ground
- The **Robot** weight must not exceed **115.0lb (52.16kg)** Not counting **Bumpers** **Battery**, or **Tags**
- Robot can not extend more the **12in (30.48cm)** beyond the vertical projection of their **Robot Perimeter**
	- Robots many only extend beyond their Perimeter in **1 direction at a time** not counting Momentary extensions such as a wire or  cable tie swinging out of the Robot Perimeter
